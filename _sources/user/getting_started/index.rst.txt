Getting started
===============

SeQuant is a general-purpose symbolic tensor algebra, but the primary use case is in quantum many-body physics and quantum chemistry. The following is a brief tutorial on
using SeQuant for this purpose: applying Wick's theorem to simple operator products, then giving indices semantic meaning by registering index
spaces, and finally composing full many-body equations out of predefined operators.
